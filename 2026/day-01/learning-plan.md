# Day 1 - Introduction to DevOps, Cloud, SRE

## What is DevOps?

DevOps is a modern approach to software development that brings development and operations teams together to deliver applications faster and more reliably.

## Why DevOps?

I want to upgrade my DevOps skills to move toward a DevOps/Cloud Engineer career.

## 3 Goals

- Build strong DevOps fundamentals.
- Deploy production-ready applications on AWS and Kubernetes.
- Get hands-on experience with Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, and Agentic AI.

## 3 Core DevOps Skills to Build

- **Linux & Troubleshooting:** System administration, processes, networking, logs, shell commands, and production troubleshooting.
- **CI/CD & Automation:** Git/GitHub, Jenkins, pipelines, automated build, test, and deployment.
- **Cloud & Infrastructure:** Docker, Kubernetes, and Terraform to provision cloud infrastructure.

## Weekly Time Budget

- **Weekdays:** 2–2.5 hours per day
- **Weekends:** 4–6 hours
